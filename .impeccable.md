## Design Context

### Users
Domain experts (software engineers, linguists, finance pros, legal experts, healthcare/sciences) searching for high-paying remote AI training jobs on Google. They want direct application to micro1 — zero recruiter friction. Context: browsing at home on desktop or mobile, comparing salaries and bonuses across 217 positions. Emotional goal: "This is the highest-paying option for my expertise and I can apply right now."

### Brand Personality
Sharp, direct, no-nonsense transactional. Three words: **mechanical, urgent, premium**. Inspired by dataannotation.tech — a landing page that converts by being lean, fast, and credible. Not friendly, not warm — just "here's the money, here's the job, click." The interface should feel like a precision instrument, not a welcoming brand.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference**: dataannotation.tech — blue `#004cf0` primary, near-black `#090909` text, green `#04c432` salary accents, 0px border radius everywhere, pixel-box canvas dividers, Space Mono for CTAs.
- **Theme**: Both light and dark via toggle. Light is the conversion priority (daytime browsing); dark is progressive enhancement.
- **Fonts**: Inter (body, explicit user choice), Hanken Grotesk (headings 500-800 weight), Space Mono (CTAs/tags). These override the AGENTS.md anti-slop rules per user direction.
- **Anti-references**: Generic "modern SaaS" templates, rounded everything, gradient text, cards with icons above headings, sticky gradient navs, Inter/Roboto-only sites.

### Design Principles
1. **Every pixel earns its click** — the design exists to drive affiliate conversions. Decorative choices that don't serve clarity or urgency are cut.
2. **Sharp is the brand** — 0px radius, high contrast, mechanical precision. Nothing soft, nothing rounded.
3. **Hierarchy through weight, not decoration** — bold salaries, dense information, clear CTAs. No illustrations, no icons that explain, no decorative flourishes that don't signal money or urgency.
4. **Dark mode is a polish, not a separate design** — all components use CSS variables so the theme flips without structural changes. Contrast at WCAG AA minimum in both modes.
5. **Motion with purpose** — pixel-box dividers signal section transitions via scroll. Card fade-ups stage information. No decorative bounce, no parallax for its own sake.
